ABOUT US

Our Story

Built for travellers who come for the hills

Welcome to our Hotel, where quality, hygiene, and taste come together in every meal. We take pride in maintaining a clean and hygienic kitchen to ensure that every dish is prepared with the highest standards of safety and freshness. We serve 100% pure vegetarian food, made with carefully selected ingredients to deliver delicious and authentic flavors. Our dedicated team is committed to preparing every order quickly and efficiently, so you can enjoy fresh, hot meals whenever you choose us. Your satisfaction, health, and memorable dining experience are always our top priorities.

Hotel Dnyaneshwari Guest House & Villa stands on the Mancher–Bhimashankar road in Pokhari, Ambegaon taluka — right along the route that most pilgrims and travellers take on their way to the Bhimashankar Jyotirlinga. We started this guest house with one goal: give people a clean, comfortable, no-fuss place to rest, so they can spend their energy on the temple, the trails and the forest around them, not on worrying about where they're staying.

Over the years we've hosted pilgrim families making the temple visit, trekking groups heading into the Sahyadri hills, and travellers simply passing through on the highway. Whoever walks in, the approach stays the same — warm, straightforward hospitality, at a fair price.

We keep two room types — Deluxe and Normal — so guests can choose what suits their trip and their budget, without unnecessary extras. Meals are not included in the room tariff, which keeps our rates honest and lets guests choose from the many local eateries nearby, including options right on the highway.
